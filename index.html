Our story

From a craving to a bakery.

It started as a simple hobby: chasing the perfect thick, gooey New York-style cookie, right here in Malta.

What began in a home kitchen quickly turned into an obsession, and eventually a full-scale bakery. Every batch is made with premium ingredients, a lot of love, and the belief that life is just better with a really good cookie.

No preservatives, no shortcuts. Just pure butter, dark brown cane sugar, high-grade chocolate chunks and that legendary melt-in-your-mouth centre.

Bakery secret

How to unlock peak gooeyness at home

Warm your cookies in a preheated oven at 160°C for 3–4 minutes (or microwave for 12–15 seconds) to bring back that molten centre.

Questions

Good to know.

How do I place an order?

Tap Build your box, pick your cookies, and we'll write the order message for you. It's copied to your clipboard and Instagram opens, so just paste it into the chat with @_madeforju.

When do you deliver?

We bake fresh and deliver across Malta at weekends. Local pickup can be arranged if you'd rather collect your box.

Can I send a box as a gift?

Yes. Turn on This is a gift in the order builder, add their name, area and a short note, and we'll deliver it to their door with your message.

How long do the cookies stay fresh?

Best within 3–4 days in an airtight container at room temperature. You can also freeze them for up to a month and reheat for a fresh-baked texture.

Do you cater for events, birthdays or weddings?

Yes! Message us on Instagram at least 2 weeks ahead so we can reserve your baking slots.
